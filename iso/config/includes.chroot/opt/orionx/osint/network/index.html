What this page is

A passive picture: the kernel's neighbour table, the vendor registry for hardware addresses, and — when Zeek is running — its conn, dns, dhcp and ssdp logs over the window you pick. Nothing here transmits a frame. Every count is observed by this deck, on the segment it is plugged into, inside that window. It is a sample of what spoke while we listened, not an inventory. Addresses are classified local or internet by RFC 1918 membership only; there is no geolocation on this page or in its data.

Headline

Protocol sightings

What kinds of chatter were heard. Present means at least one record in the window; absent means the log that would show it was read and held none; unknown means that log is not available on this deck, and the row says which. IoT is a heuristic from vendor prefixes and mDNS service names, never an identification.

Neighbour table (ARP / NDP)

Hardware addresses this deck has exchanged frames with, from ip neigh. Vendor is a registry lookup of the 24-bit prefix (ieee-data); a randomised address has no vendor and is marked so. These entries age by the kernel's rules, not by the window above.

Operator-initiated

Active checks, beneath the passive picture because they are not passive. Each is started by you and says what it sent. Gateway identity transmits on this segment only; every value it shows is reported by the gateway, not measured. Upstream probes reach the internet: each is a probe, posture-gated by the same rule as GODSEYE, and at a blocked posture you see the refusal, not a result.
